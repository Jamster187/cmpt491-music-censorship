# Exhaustive regression on expanded features, V2

This is an **exploratory prediction campaign**, authorized after feature expansion V1 (`1414f21`). The 2018-onward evaluation period has already been inspected in earlier work. It is not fresh independent confirmation. The estimand is future chart performance of an exact original Billboard title/full artist-credit identity, conditional on entering the chart. It does not describe songs that never charted or establish causal effects.

## Prediction moment and feature availability

The prediction moment is immediately after the identity's first observed Hot 100 chart is published. A source chart date is a chart label, not a recording release date or an independently verified publication timestamp. Same-chart information is allowed because the entire first chart is assumed available then. Earlier-history features use strictly earlier source charts. The supplied snapshot's reported upstream is [mhollingshead/billboard-hot-100](https://github.com/mhollingshead/billboard-hot-100); this attribution is not independent verification.

The strict track includes 37 original variables: five eligible original chart/date variables and 32 new constructs. The retrospective sensitivity includes 108: 51 original and all 57 new constructs. It was explicitly authorized in addition to the strict analysis. Retrospective results are **modern measurement associations, not historically deployable debut-time forecasts**. Missing historical availability for lyrics, classifiers, genre, duration and entity crosswalks remains a limitation even when their chart-history joins use earlier dates.

The [predictor inventory](predictor_inventory.csv) lists every included variable, source field, family, availability and encoding. The [source-column audit](source_column_eligibility.csv) accounts for columns across original song and weekly exports, V1 features and V1 outcomes. No correlation screening or outcome-based shortlist is used. All 42 existing classifier scores, primary genre, secondary-genre set, annotation confidence and accepted consistent duration enter the retrospective original universe. Confidence is a measurement-process proxy. Modern accumulated recording/release counts, acquisition statuses, identifiers, later chart summaries, outcome fields and uncertain release-date proxies are excluded. Multiple duration summaries are aliases of the one accepted duration on the V1 consistency mask, not additional predictors.

Original chart variables are debut rank, a deterministic continuous debut date, and three values reported on the **first** published chart: previous-week rank, peak-so-far and reported weeks-on-chart. Rare nonempty previous-week values, weeks above one and peak/debut-rank disagreements are preserved source identity/history anomalies. They neither merge identities nor license using any later observation. Audit counts are retained. Peak-so-far is often a duplicate of debut rank, and its combinations are flagged. No variable mathematically reconstructs a future target.

New features retain V1 definitions. Season sine and cosine form **one circular construct**, with two encoded columns. One categorical or multilabel construct counts as one original variable regardless of width. Calendar weekday and continuous time are available, but an official sales/radio/streaming methodology regime is **not supplied**: V1 deferred it because a validated historical crosswalk was unavailable. This campaign does not invent such a regime or acquire external metadata. Exact full-credit artist history does not resolve individual people or acts; entity-level alternatives remain retrospective.

## Outcomes and censoring

Three numerical targets are assessed in each of two outcome windows: best rank (minimum rank; lower is better), distinct chart dates, and chart points (sum of `101 − weekly_rank`). All source observations, including repeated song/date rows, contribute to points; distinct dates define weeks. No duplicate is silently removed from the source. Both windows include the first chart, which is already known at prediction time. This is total performance after first publication, not a target with the debut contribution subtracted.

**Complete 26-week windows are the primary forward-looking comparison.** They cover `[first_chart_date, first_chart_date + 182 days)`, exactly as in V1. Eligibility requires the global last source chart to reach the window end. A training window must finish by the training cutoff; a validation window must finish by its period endpoint. Thus late incomplete debuts and windows crossing a cutoff are excluded consistently for every candidate. Future chart disappearance is not required. Historical/source coverage and omissions can still limit measurement.

**Observed-lifetime outcomes are a censored-history sensitivity, not verified eventual completed careers.** At every cutoff separately, the weekly data are first truncated to dates strictly before that cutoff. Labels and eligibility are then computed from that prefix. An identity must have debuted at least five calendar years earlier and have no observed chart appearance in the last two calendar years. The same rule applies to historical training, validation and final evaluation. Later observations cannot determine historical inclusion or historical labels. Final labels use the supplied source endpoint, with an exclusive cutoff on the following day.

This maturity/inactivity rule selects a different, older cohort and does not solve lifetime right-censoring: songs can return after long absences. Historical return diagnostics quantify that limitation. Claims about true eventual lifetime performance would require a different survival/censoring design and are not made. Do not compare its target scores directly against the 26-week cohort as if the population were identical. There is no equivalent V1 lifetime model. The five-year maturity and two-year inactivity thresholds are frozen descriptive eligibility choices, not estimated natural boundaries or a COVID breakpoint.

## Populations and splits

Source assets use deterministic exact title/artist IDs, never normalized merge keys. The source feature universe contains 32,723 chart identities; the strict track retains post-1960 debuts, including identities outside the final monthly lyric/classifier study. The retrospective cohort additionally requires all 42 frozen scores and parsed nonempty lyric features, reproducing V1's selection. This selected content cohort is not interchangeable with the strict chart population. Missing duration or other predictors never triggers candidate-specific deletion.

Rolling validation periods are 1990–1999, 2000–2009 and 2010–2017. Each model trains only on eligible labels available at that period's start. The first source years provide artist-history burn-in; focal debuts begin in 1960. All candidates within a task share exactly the same train and validation identities. [Cohort hashes](cohorts.csv) and [fold metadata](fold_metadata.json) preserve dates, sizes, baselines, unknown-category counts and IDs digests.

The final evaluation contains eligible debuts from 2018 onward. Every task's validation results and original/new/combined winners, both overall and at each variable count, are locked in [selection_lock.json](selection_lock.json) **before any final candidate evaluation**. Then every candidate is refitted on eligible labels available before 2018 and evaluated on the same task population. Evaluation scores never rerank predictor hierarchies or choose a winner. Reporting the maximum evaluation score among all candidates is a negative-result/coverage audit only, not a model recommendation.

## Exhaustive OLS and ranking

The search enumerates all unordered additive combinations of one, two or three original variables. There are 8,473 strict combinations and 210,042 retrospective combinations per outcome. Six outcomes per track yield 1,311,090 distinct task/candidate pairs. Three validation folds plus final refitting yield 5,244,360 candidate fits. Original-only and new-only comparisons filter this same complete catalog; they are not extra duplicate searches. Conceptual overlaps and rank-deficient combinations are retained, scored and flagged.

OLS includes an intercept, without interactions, coefficient regularization or prediction clipping. Training-only preprocessing uses medians, with zero for an entirely missing numeric training field, and no missingness indicators. Numeric and encoded categorical columns are centered and scaled on training rows. Categorical vocabularies come from training observations, drop one reference level, and map missing/unseen categories to the all-zero raw block. Secondary genres use a training-only multilabel vocabulary. Constant encoded columns are removed while their original-variable combinations remain. Consequently some conceptually different models have identical fitted predictions, and unknown or missing categories can be conflated with the reference category. These are declared encoding limits, not held-out fitted information.

Training Gram matrices and augmented QR factors are cached. Batched eigensolutions handle well-conditioned submatrices; near-singular or rank-deficient designs use cached QR/SVD with a full-training-row rank tolerance. Independent full-row SciPy SVD checks cover numerical and categorical blocks, duplicate columns and selected models. MAE and squared errors are computed against all rows, without a sampled metric approximation. Training R² uses cached cross-products; its additional cached coefficient solve is an implementation operation, not another distinct model.

Selection maximizes the **equal-period mean MSE skill against each fold's training-mean baseline**, then prefers fewer original variables and the lexicographic predictor tuple. Validation R² and MAE are equal-period arithmetic means. Reported validation RMSE is the square root of equal-period mean MSE, not pooled-row RMSE or mean RMSE. Each fold's individual metrics are preserved. Within-count and original/new/combined ranks follow the same validation rule. Final target leaderboards use evaluation MSE skill only to compare target predictability within a track, horizon and configuration after each target's model is fixed. Different target variances and selected populations still constrain interpretation.

Every candidate has validation/evaluation R², MAE, RMSE, baseline access, sample sizes, fold-specific performance, worst-period skill, period range, and evaluation-minus-validation/training gaps. Negative R² means worse than the evaluation-population mean; negative baseline-relative skill means worse than the frozen training mean. A positive skill can coexist with negative R² under a shifted outcome distribution. Poor performance is not evidence that outcomes are random.

## Comparisons and uncertainty

Original-only, new-only and combined results use identical target definitions, cohorts, folds and preprocessing rules. Training-mean, training-median and fitted one-variable debut-rank OLS baselines are reported. The mean is the squared-error baseline; the median is the absolute-error baseline. The retained kernel uses the lower weighted median (equal song weights); this is an absolute-error minimizer even when an even-sized sample has two middle values. New features are not credited for merely outperforming an intentionally weak lyrics-only comparator.

V1 comparisons use its previously validation-selected predictions, never whichever V1 model happens to score best on evaluation. All three 26-week targets must match evaluation IDs and actual values exactly. Two V2 refits are shown: the primary pre-2018 refit, which benefits from more recent training, and an additional refit of the already selected V2 variable combination on the **exact V1 pre-2010 training identities**. The latter isolates training-population differences, but model classes, candidate spaces and selection protocols still differ. It is not a pure causal effect of adding features. No V1 comparison is asserted for observed-lifetime targets.

For overall original/new/combined winners, 499 seeded paired resamples provide percentile intervals for R², MAE, RMSE, training-mean skill, and improvements relative to original-only predictions. Resampling units are exact full artist credit and, separately, debut year. Artist-credit groups do not resolve collaboration aliases; year intervals involve few evaluation years. These fixed-prediction intervals account for some dependence and pairing, but **exclude training, refitting, exhaustive model-selection and repeated-test-inspection uncertainty**. They are descriptive, not multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory intervals. Selected coefficients are conditional on a large search and must not be treated as unbiased causal estimates.

Feature-family frequency counts whether a family appears in each selected task winner, and separately counts its variables. The six outcomes are correlated; these frequencies are not independent replications, importance estimates or causal effects. Focused inclusion/exclusion summaries select both sides using validation alone. Their evaluation differences are exploratory comparisons between different models, not controlled feature ablations.

## Execution, storage and provenance

The protocol, complete inventory, data hashes, runtime versions and selection rule were frozen before fitting. An outcome-blind stratified benchmark covers variable count, categorical presence, both windows and all validation periods. A conservative runtime projection must fit the frozen two-hour launch budget; otherwise execution checkpoints before launch. Disk space retains at least 3 GiB headroom. No final evaluation labels are benchmarked.

Each task has a local SQLite catalog and transactional batches of 256 scores. Resume identity includes numerical code, input hashes, environment, task and exact candidate catalog. Mismatches fail; committed batches are skipped and incomplete batches are recomputed. All poor and singular candidates remain stored. Complete compressed hierarchies and numeric selected predictions remain under ignored `data/experiments/regression_expanded_v2/`, with public row-count/hash manifests. Small hierarchies, scores, model encoders/coefficients, documentation and SVG figures are versioned. No lyrics, private databases or millions of fitted model objects are published.

The preservation snapshot covers all pre-existing tracked files and local data/analysis artifacts. Validation verifies their original hashes, all candidate/fold coverage, frozen selections, direct regression calculations, exact V1 populations, model replays, deterministic result regeneration and interruption recovery. Source data, prior regression campaigns and feature-expansion V1 remain unchanged. Root navigation files are frozen dependencies of earlier publications, so this campaign adds its own entry document without modifying them.
