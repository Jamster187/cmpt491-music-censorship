# Cross-domain regression discovery V3 methodology

This campaign asks which existing variables predict other variables from different information domains. It is not restricted to commercial forecasting. It uses the existing expanded dataset, existing numerical/date encodings and frozen classifier/genre measurements. No data acquisition, feature construction, classification, clustering, association mining or COVID analysis is part of this campaign.

The 2018-onward evaluation period was inspected in earlier research. All conclusions are **exploratory**. Chronological separation prevents identity overlap and retrospective model selection on evaluation metrics; it does not turn modern measurements into historically available data or make this a fresh independent confirmation.

## Inventory and measurement units

The complete inventory contains 129 predictor constructs, including all 57 newly engineered constructs from feature expansion V1. There are 125 meaningful numerical target candidates. The data-only support audit admits 123 targets: debut-reported prior-week rank has no observed target values in the common enriched cohort, and debut-reported weeks-on-chart is constant in the historical validation cohorts. These two remain documented and remain predictor candidates; constant encoded columns do not remove their conceptual combinations.

The [target inventory](target_inventory.csv), [predictor inventory](predictor_inventory.csv), [source-column inventory](source_column_inventory.csv) and [directed relationship audit](relationship_pairs.csv) provide definitions and dispositions. Inventory decisions use documented meanings, dependencies and observed support, never correlations or model scores. Every numerical target must have at least 100 training and 30 validation identities and nonconstant observed values in each of three validation periods. Evaluation performance never determines eligibility.

Targets include 42 classifier scores; lyric length, repetition, complexity and language-use measurements; recording-duration and retained-catalog measurements; exact-credit and entity-based artist histories; chart context and commercial outcomes; meaningful title/credit indicators; and historical/calendar measurements. Existing 0/1 indicators are retained as numerical targets. Their OLS predictions are unclipped linear-probability diagnostics, not calibrated classifiers. Calendar weekday codes, one-hot category numbers, and separate sine/cosine coordinates are not meaningful scalar targets. Season sine and cosine remain one joint predictor construct.

MusicBrainz min/median/max duration summaries describe retained recording manifestations, which may disagree. They are not exact duplicates over the whole original source population. V1's consistent-duration field uses a smaller, stricter population. All are inventoried, but predicting one duration measurement from another is excluded as reconstruction of the same physical measurement. Retained recording/release/distinct-duration counts measure incomplete, sometimes capped catalog coverage; they do not measure true total versions, artist quality or intrinsic musical complexity. Existing earliest-release-year encoding is not a verified original release year. No partial date is expanded into an invented month/day.

IDs, source-row locations, acquisition/availability statuses, annotation confidence, monthly selection coordinates/counts and window/censoring flags are excluded as predictors and substantive targets. Original full-history rank/weeks/points map to the existing canonical observed-history columns. Source row count is redundant observational bookkeeping; distinct chart dates are the canonical chart-week measurement. First-chart date has one existing V2 numerical representation. Last-chart date and retained release year reuse the original campaign's encodings. No new feature is engineered for this search.

## Populations and time

The unit is one exact original title/full artist-credit identity. Raw title and artist strings and deterministic IDs are retained. Different collaborations are different credits, not resolved people. No identity or arbitrary artist code enters OLS.

The common base is V2's retrospective enriched cohort: post-1960 debuts with all 42 existing scores and nonempty existing lyric structure. It contains 20,508 identities before target/window filtering. This avoids letting absent lyric acquisition or final monthly-selection status become an implicit predictor of chart success. It remains a selected enriched cohort, not the entire Billboard population. Missing metadata and some structural/history measurements still receive training-only imputation; selection and provider-coverage effects remain possible.

All populations require the existing complete 26-week window so that both existing 12-week and 26-week predictors can be measured. A window must end by the relevant partition cutoff. Training and validation rows additionally require an observed target; targets are never imputed. Top-10 targets retain V1's exclusion of debuts already in the Top 10. Each target's exact train/validation/evaluation identities are identical across original-only, new-only, combined, classifier-only and all relationship views. A target with sparse observed metadata therefore uses a different population from a fully observed content target; cross-target comparisons acknowledge this.

Validation periods are 1990–1999, 2000–2009 and 2010–2017. Each trains only on earlier eligible chart labels. Final refits use eligible pre-2018 training identities, and evaluation uses eligible debuts from 2018 onward. Entire identities stay in one chronological partition. Modern lyrics, classifiers, genre and linked metadata remain retrospective measurements even when their chart-reference histories are truncated correctly.

Observed-history predictors and targets use the existing V2 rule separately at every cutoff: truncate chart observations strictly before the cutoff, then require age of at least five calendar years and inactivity of at least two years. Other rows have missing observed-history predictors; observed-history targets require this eligibility. The rule does not establish completed careers or eliminate future returns. Dates are chart labels, not release dates or independently verified publication timestamps. `observed_return_after_26weeks` means any observed appearance at or after 26 weeks, including continuous charting; it does not require an absence and re-entry.

A combination is timing-compatible with a debut forecast only when the target is a future fixed-window outcome and every predictor is available at debut. Such a label does not remove retrospective enriched-cohort selection. Models using post-debut commercial predictors or modern content/metadata measurements are retrospective relationships. V3's primary scientific-interest models are not marketed as deployable forecasts.

## Relationship policy

The [frozen protocol](protocol.json) defines pairwise categories, explicit reconstruction rules and combination precedence before the full search. Different column names do not imply independent measurement.

| Category | Meaning | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Direct reconstruction, self-target, or explicit target dependency | Fully enumerated in the audit; excluded from fitting and valid selection |
| B | Same substantive measurement family | Retained in unrestricted valid results; flagged as expected/related measurement |
| C | Different families with a prespecified conceptual overlap or obvious proxy | Retained in unrestricted and broad different-family audit; excluded from genuine cross-domain views |
| D | Distinct domains with shared recorded measurement provenance | Retained in cross-domain results; not called provenance-independent |
| E | Distinct domains, no dependency/overlap rule, and disjoint recorded measurement-source sets | Primary provenance-separated view |

Any A rule makes the entire combination A. Otherwise the least-separated target/predictor pair determines the combination. A model mixing same-family and independent predictors remains B. Independence among the predictors themselves is not assumed; redundant or correlated predictors and rank-deficient fits remain represented. One categorical/multilabel predictor counts as one variable regardless of encoded width.

A rules include self-target use; different encodings of the same calendar state or career endpoints; alternate physical-duration measurements; the same commercial measurement across nested windows or threshold flags; any focal score reused in a content-distance feature; explicit scalar reuse in selected lyric formulas; focal genre reused in focal genre-context features; career endpoint/post-window proxies; and the other two sentiment probabilities jointly reconstructing the third. These are definition-based guards, not empirical correlation cutoffs. Conservative same-measurement exclusions are distinguished from universal mathematical equality.

Genre annotation provenance needs particular care. Its frozen prompt uses Billboard identity and **first chart date**, MusicBrainz/Wikidata evidence and learned genre knowledge. It forbids lyrics, content scores and chart performance. Consequently genre is not provenance-separated from Billboard or MusicBrainz. Using focal genre or its directly dependent context to predict first-chart date is A because that date was already an annotation input. Different lyric classifiers and lyric structure share the same lyric source; they are not provenance-separated merely because their algorithms or columns differ. Content-distance features explicitly reuse the focal classifier vector and cannot validly predict one of its components.

Conceptual overlaps include content/affect/lyric structure, title-language proxies, duration versus lyric word/vocabulary counts, release-year versus chart-date placement, collaboration markers versus credited-entity counts, exact-credit versus entity histories, genre versus derived genre contexts, and debut rank versus later chart performance. Strict separation is conservative and may exclude useful but substantively overlapping relationships.

Category E means separation of the **recorded measurement pipelines**, not statistical independence, independent sampling, proof about unknown pretraining corpora, or causality. All rows share asset linkage and chart/enrichment selection. Genre model knowledge, imperfect recording matches, provider coverage, era, language, artist popularity and collaboration aliases can still confound E relationships.

## Exhaustive enumeration and estimation

The common catalog contains all 357,889 unordered one-, two- and three-variable combinations from the 129 constructs, including combinations containing each target for the complete A audit. Per-target relationship codes and rule-bit arrays retain every exclusion. The 123 supported targets have 40,884,781 valid task/candidate pairs, requiring 122,654,343 validation fits and 40,884,781 final-evaluation fits: **163,539,124 fits**. Original/new/classifier-only and relationship views are filters of this same exhaustive search, not extra duplicate searches. No greedy search, stepwise selection, correlation screening or heuristic shortlist is used.

The estimator is additive OLS with an intercept, no interactions or regularization and no prediction clipping. Training-only preprocessing uses median imputation (zero when entirely missing), standardization, and categories/multilabel vocabulary observed in training. Unseen and missing categories map to the all-zero raw reference block. Constant encoded columns are dropped, while their conceptual combinations remain. Missing and reference levels can be conflated; no explicit availability flag is added. Targets and target-dependent A predictors are omitted from each target's design matrix entirely.

The preserved validated kernel caches Gram matrices and augmented QR factors. Batched eigensolutions handle well-conditioned combinations; cached QR/SVD handles near-singular and rank-deficient cases with the existing full-row rank tolerance. V3 computes exact held-out residuals using sparse coefficient matrices and contiguous cached feature columns, avoiding multiplication by coefficients known to be zero. It uses every held-out row and every categorical coefficient. Dense-kernel and independent full-row SVD comparisons verify the optimization.

Selection maximizes **equal-period mean validation R²**, then prefers fewer original variables and the lexicographic predictor tuple. This differs from V2's mean-baseline-relative MSE-skill selection and is frozen for V3's cross-target comparison. Validation MAE is the equal-period mean; validation RMSE is the square root of equal-period mean MSE. Full period-level metrics remain available. Training-mean-relative MSE skill, training-median-relative MAE skill and evaluation-minus-validation gaps are reported separately. The median baseline uses the retained kernel's lower weighted median with equal song weights.

All target/view/configuration winners at each variable count and overall, plus the global leading relationships, are locked using validation alone before any final evaluation. Every valid candidate then receives final evaluation metrics on the same target population. Global relationship leaderboards rank by validation R²; they are not reranked by evaluation performance. A separate best-per-target hierarchy avoids confusing many variants of one relationship with many distinct discoveries. Negative and singular results remain stored.

## Original versus new information

Original-only includes pre-existing measurements outside the 57 new constructs, including existing commercial targets reused as retrospective predictors and original metadata summaries. New-only uses the 57 engineered constructs when allowed by the target's relationship policy. Combined uses their union. Classifier-only filters the same catalog to the 42 existing scores. It can have no eligible provenance-separated combinations for a lyric-derived target; such cases are explicitly unavailable, not replaced with a leaky comparison.

Configuration comparisons always use identical target definitions, identity populations, folds and preprocessing rules. Differences between selected winners are predictive comparisons between searches, not causal feature effects. Family frequencies count occurrence in winners across correlated targets; they are neither independent replications nor unbiased importance estimates. A calendar or catalog-coverage association is not automatically an interesting discovery.

## Uncertainty, stability and substantive interpretation

Leading relationships receive paired fixed-prediction resampling by exact artist credit and by debut year, using 499 seeded draws. These intervals capture some dependence and pairing but exclude refitting, model-search selection, repeated evaluation inspection and unobserved alias resolution. Few evaluation years limit year-cluster inference. No confirmatory significance or multiplicity-adjusted discovery claim is made.

Reports retain each validation period, evaluation performance and baseline improvement, along with recorded-source separation, likely confounders, possible reverse direction and a substantive-surprise assessment. Positive average validation R² alone is insufficient. Descriptive reliability checks look for positive R² in every historical validation period and in evaluation, and compare results with baselines and uncertainty. These checks annotate already fixed models; they never select a replacement based on evaluation. Within-debut-year diagnostics and seen/unseen exact-credit evaluation distinguish some era or credit-reuse patterns from broader association without changing the fitted model.

## Compute bounds and artifacts

A bounded benchmark measures every supported target and validation period, stratified by variable count and categorical-block presence. It never evaluates final-period models. Projection includes final-evaluation row counts, checkpoint I/O, preprocessing, contingency and reporting/validation allowance. Full execution requires a projection no greater than **four hours** and **15 GB**, plus physical disk headroom; otherwise the campaign stops after its actual audit and benchmark for approval.

Complete machine-readable hierarchies use float64 arrays storing every candidate's MAE/MSE for all four periods, with exact fold constants for deriving R²/RMSE/baseline skill. Rank/fallback codes, complete relationship/reconstruction arrays, the global predictor catalog, valid-ID mapping and deterministic validation order make every candidate recoverable. This avoids storing duplicate multi-gigabyte CSVs or millions of full fitted models. A query/export command exposes ordinary tabular rows.

Checkpoints commit checksummed chunks of 8,192 candidates after array flush/fsync and a durable SQLite ledger transaction. Uncommitted chunks are rerun; committed chunks are verified and skipped. Code, input, environment, protocol and candidate identity mismatches fail. Prior datasets, code and results—including all of V2—are protected by a separate preservation snapshot. Large arrays, local predictions and intermediate stores remain ignored. Only actual completed summaries, documentation, selected portable models and figures are published.
