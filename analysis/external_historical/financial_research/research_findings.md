# Findings from financial and macroeconomic discovery

The best practical starting point is a small, interpretable combination of **labor stress, inflation, market returns/drawdowns, borrowing costs, disposable income and consumer perceptions**. Additional financial-stress indexes, household hardship surveys, uncertainty measures and distributional data add useful distinctions, often with much shorter histories. These are proposed information channels, not discovered correlations with our music data.

## Coverage and source conclusions

**Equities and regimes.** French broad-market excess return plus its matching risk-free return offers a long-run research route to total market returns, distinct from a literal S&P 500 series. Shiller offers monthly-average composite prices and historical earnings/dividends, not month-end closes. FRED's S&P 500/Dow mirrors have rolling historical limits, and Nasdaq, Nasdaq-100, Russell and VIX cannot supply actual 1958 observations. Licensed constituent history is needed for defensible breadth, turnover and concentration. The French FIZ-to-CIZ transition also changes calculation conventions. [French library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html), [S&P 500 metadata](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SP500), [Shiller](https://www.econ.yale.edu/~shiller/data.htm).

**Stress and monetary conditions.** Long-run Baa/Aaa/Treasury spreads offer coverage but mix duration, credit quality and liquidity. Modern NFCI, STLFSI, KCFSI, OFR and excess bond premium measure related but different conditions; none should be treated as five independent shocks. Historical estimates often predate publication of the index itself. VIX is implied volatility; realized volatility is computed from returns. LIBOR/OIS, SOFR and Treasury discount yields require instrument/basis discipline. QE/QT policy announcements are distinct from changes in total Fed assets. [NFCI](https://www.chicagofed.org/research/data/nfci/current-data), [STLFSI4](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/STLFSI4), [excess bond premium](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/updating-the-recession-risk-and-the-excess-bond-premium-20161006.html), [Fed policy communications](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/policy-normalization.htm).

**Growth, prices and fiscal conditions.** GDP/GDI, investment, profits, productivity and fiscal measures add broad economic context. Quarterly/annual values should keep their native frequency. CPI components are overlapping inputs, not independent price shocks; the final-demand PPI identifier is `PPIFIS`, not the similarly named intermediate-demand series. GDP, seasonal adjustments, population benchmarks and potential output are revised. Fiscal-year budgets, tax-year data and calendar-year GDP require an explicit alignment rule. [BEA](https://apps.bea.gov/api/signup/), [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/developers/), [PPI metadata](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/PPIFIS), [budget historical tables](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/BUDGET-2027-TAB/context).

**Household stress and inequality.** Youth unemployment, real wages, savings, delinquency, debt service, food insecurity and financial expectations capture different exposures. The current household debt-service series begins in 2005 under a new method; the old-method 1980–2024 series is a separate historical object. SCF/DFA, SHED, SCE, food-insecurity and homelessness series are mainly modern or low-frequency. Mobility cohorts, generation wealth and regional indicators need care: a US chart does not identify each listener's geography, generation or economic condition. [Debt-service methodological change](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/dsr/about.htm), [SHED](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/shed_data.htm), [DFA](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/z1/dataviz/dfa/index.html), [USDA](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-security-in-the-united-states).

**Market psychology deserves its own block.** Consumer sentiment and expectations, economic-news sentiment, Yale crash confidence, Baker–Wurgler sentiment, AAII shares, margin debt, fund flows and job-loss expectations are not interchangeable. Some measure stated beliefs, some positioning, and some constructed residuals. AAII currently advertises full historical data as a membership benefit; old public-download descriptions are insufficient. Modern fear/greed products lack a credible full-history route. [Michigan tables](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/tables.html), [AAII](https://www.aaii.com/sentimentsurvey), [SF Fed news sentiment](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/data-and-indicators/daily-news-sentiment-index/), [Yale confidence](https://som.yale.edu/centers/international-center-for-finance/data/stock-market-confidence-indices/united-states).

**International and commodity context.** Gold, oil, food/metals, the dollar, trade, supply-chain stress, sanctions and geopolitical risk may add external channels. Most contemporary commodity benchmarks and floating FX histories do not span 1958. Fixed-price gold, dual-market arrangements and later floating prices must not be treated as one stationary exposure. Global series also differ from US household experience. [World Bank commodity documentation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets), [EIA API](https://www.eia.gov/opendata/), [BIS portal](https://data.bis.org/), [GPR](https://www.matteoiacoviello.com/gpr.htm).

**Uncertainty and additional discoveries.** Macro/financial uncertainty, longitudinal family income, financial satisfaction, leisure time and generation-specific wealth emerged from the final catalog/literature passes. The current Ludvigson uncertainty release explicitly removes certain COVID-related spikes; that processing choice is consequential for this repository's eventual question. Its older vintages must remain available for review. FRED-MD/QD was used as a catalog cross-check, not imported wholesale: its transformations and frequency variants do not automatically add new independent constructs. [Uncertainty documentation](https://www.sydneyludvigson.com/macro-and-financial-uncertainty-indexes), [PSID](https://psidonline.isr.umich.edu/), [GSS](https://gssdataexplorer.norc.org/gss_data), [FRED-MD/QD](https://www.stlouisfed.org/research/economists/mccracken/fred-databases).

## Most useful comparisons to propose

- **Drawdown versus lyrical sentiment:** broad-market total-return drawdown and realized volatility, with price-drawdown alternatives explicitly separated. Compare these with emotion dimensions and the compensatory-listening hypothesis; no expected sign is assumed.
- **Household hardship versus lyrical anger/comfort:** unemployment, youth unemployment, inflation, real wage/income growth and savings; modern extensions add delinquency, food insecurity and expected job loss.
- **Perceptions versus realized conditions:** consumer sentiment/news sentiment/job-loss expectations alongside actual labor and income changes. This distinguishes beliefs from measured outcomes.
- **Financial stress versus ordinary market fluctuations:** EBP or one broad conditions index plus a long-run credit spread; avoid simultaneously counting all stress composites as independent evidence.
- **Genre composition and commercial prominence:** age-specific labor conditions, income/wealth distribution and recreation spending. The proposed channel is audience resources/composition; aggregate data cannot prove listener-level mechanisms.

The [literature](academic_literature.md) supports both hardship-expression and compensatory-consumption hypotheses and shows that music–market associations can fail to persist. Rankings therefore reflect source quality, coverage and distinct measurement channels, not promised correlations or percentage gains. [Temporal alignment](temporal_alignment.md) states the required distinctions before any statistical plan is chosen.

Search expansion stopped after the final official catalog, household/mobility, uncertainty and market-psychology passes yielded mainly existing constructs, alternate frequencies, proprietary products or unresolved archives. This is a broad practical research universe, not a claim that every financial variable has been enumerated. The remaining work is exact endpoint/rights/vintage validation for a reviewed subset, not bulk acquisition of all rows.
